## Selected projects

### [Colombia Crime Analytics AI Agent](https://github.com/eserrania/agente_victimas_colombia)
An AI agent that answers questions about crime and victim data in Colombia, querying official government open data sources in real time.

### [Colombian Armed Conflict Victim Forecasting](https://github.com/eserrania/armed_conflict_forecasting)
A model that forecasts the number of victims of the armed conflict in Colombia at the department level for the current year using data from the Colombian Victims Unit (Unidad para las Víctimas) open-data portal.

### [Interactive Visualization of homicide rates in Mexican municipalities (2011-2019)](https://eserrania.github.io/homicide_scatterplot/)
Using d3.js, I designed an interactive scatterplot showing the evolution of homicide rates at the Municipal level.
